# 1:1 test prints – check the real parts before ordering

Generated by `make_print_parts.py` (KiCad's Python) and `make_spacers.py` from the real board files, so every hole is
where the ordered PCBs will have it.

| File | What | Print |
|---|---|---|
| `pcb_left.stl`, `pcb_column.stl` | PCB stand-ins with all holes (switch pins, encoder lugs, LED, OLED header, stack holes) | 1.6 mm |
| `plate_left.stl`, `plate_column1.stl` | switch plate section | 1.6 mm as exported; **scale Z to 1.5 mm** (MX clips are made for 1.5) |
| `front_left.stl`, `front_column1.stl` | front panel section (cut-outs; legends are only print on the real panel) | 1.6 mm |
| `spacer_3.5mm.stl` | PCB -> plate (print 4 per section) | |
| `spacer_5.0mm.stl` | plate -> front panel (the 5 mm Würth standoff) | |

Sections: left block (panel x 0-103.2 mm) and column 1 (x 103.2-194.2 mm), cut in the gaps between the groups.
Stack: PCB stand-in, 3.5 mm spacers, plate, 5.0 mm spacers, front panel, held with M2 screws (or pins) through the
2.2 mm stack holes.

What to check with the real parts (the VERIFY items of `../README.md`):
1. MX switches clip into the plate and sit straight; the 18 mm caps move freely in the front cut-outs (1 mm around).
2. MEC 3FTH9: the 4 switch pins **and the 2 LED pins** go into the holes (LED pin positions are read from the drawing).
   The 1T cap moves freely in the 11 x 11 mm hole and sticks out ~3.3 mm.
3. EC11E: pins and lugs fit, the shaft is centred in the 7.5 mm hole, the body passes the plate.
4. Newhaven: pins in the header row, 2.5 mm spacer below it, glass just under the front panel, active area centred in
   the window.
5. Cap heights: how far the big caps stick out (expected ~8 mm with an 18 mm cap; lower profile if too high).

FDM holes come out ~0.1-0.2 mm small: use the slicer's hole compensation (e.g. "XY hole compensation 0.15 mm") or the
fit is tighter than on the real FR4 parts.
